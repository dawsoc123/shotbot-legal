ShotBot is a personal, non-commercial tool that its owner runs on their own computer. It turns highlights of Polish GTA 6 live streams into short clips. Only when the owner clicks “upload” does it send a clip to the owner's own YouTube channel or to the owner's TikTok drafts, using the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Who can use it

ShotBot is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users or accounts.

2. Connected accounts

The owner connects their TikTok or YouTube account with the official login screen and can disconnect it at any time, in ShotBot (“Disconnect”) or in the TikTok or Google account settings.

3. Content

Every clip is reviewed by the owner before it is uploaded. On TikTok, each video goes to the drafts inbox and is published manually in the TikTok app. Each clip credits the original streamer and links to their channel.

4. Platform rules

Use of ShotBot follows the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google API Services terms.

5. No warranty

ShotBot is provided “as is”, without any warranty.
