Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ShotBot runs only on its owner's computer. It has no servers, no website accounts and no other users.

Data ShotBot uses

  • TikTok: your basic profile (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and permission to upload videos to your drafts (scope video.upload).
  • YouTube / Google: permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) and to read your channel name (scope youtube.readonly).

How it is stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are saved only in a local file on the owner's computer.
  • They are sent only to the official TikTok and Google APIs, never anywhere else.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Deleting your data

Click “Disconnect” in ShotBot to delete the stored tokens, or revoke access in your TikTok or Google account settings. Uploaded videos can be deleted in the TikTok or YouTube app.
