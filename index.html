shotbot

Personal desktop application · Windows

shotbot is a personal, non-commercial desktop application that I run on my own computer. It watches public Polish GTA 6 live streams on Twitch and Kick, saves the moments where the chat reacts the most, and turns them into short vertical clips (9:16) with Polish subtitles.

What it is for

  • Find the best moments of GTA 6 streams automatically, based on chat reactions.
  • Edit them into YouTube Shorts / TikTok format with subtitles and a credit to the streamer.
  • Let me review every clip by hand in a local control panel.
  • Upload a clip I approved to my own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API) or to my own TikTok drafts (TikTok Content Posting API), only when I click the upload button.

Accounts and data

I connect my YouTube and TikTok accounts with the official Google and TikTok login screens. The access tokens stay only on my computer and can be removed at any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
